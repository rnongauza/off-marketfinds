SMS terms

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Off-Market Finds, operated by Kendrick Realty Inc. (Nongauza Real Estate Group), sends text messages to people who sign up for the service or text our number first.

What you’ll receive

Replies about the properties you create (for example a request for the asking price, the link to your new deal page, and photo import updates) and messages about your account. No marketing messages.

How to opt in

By checking the text-message box when you sign up, or by texting a property address or START to our number.

Frequency and cost

Message frequency varies with how often you use the service. Message and data rates may apply.

Help and opting out

Reply HELP for help, or contact robert@kendrickrealtyinc.com or 510-459-3029. Reply STOP to stop receiving messages at any time; you’ll receive one confirmation and nothing more. Reply START to resubscribe.

Privacy

Mobile numbers and opt-in information are never shared with or sold to third parties for marketing. See our Privacy policy.
