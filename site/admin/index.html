How to get a share link
  1. Google Drive: right-click the folder › Share › General access › “Anyone with the link” › Copy link.
  2. Dropbox: hover the folder › Share › Copy link.
  3. iPhone / iCloud: Photos › Shared Albums › open the album › People icon › turn on “Public Website” › Share Link.
  4. Google Photos: open the album › Share › Create link.
A short off-marketfinds.com/go/… link is made for it when you save.
